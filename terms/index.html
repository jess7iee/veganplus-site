🌱 Vegan+ 用户协议

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

中文English隐私政策 / Privacy支持 / Support

1. 接受协议

注册或使用 Vegan+ 社区，即表示你同意本协议和隐私政策。

2. 零容忍：禁止的内容

Vegan+ 对令人反感的内容和滥用行为零容忍。禁止发布：

  • 仇恨、歧视、骚扰、威胁或霸凌他人的内容；
  • 色情、暴力、血腥或其他不当内容；
  • 垃圾信息、广告、诈骗，或冒充他人；
  • 违法内容，或侵犯他人版权、隐私的内容；
  • 可能危害健康的虚假信息。

3. 举报与处理

你可以举报任何帖子或评论，也可以屏蔽任何用户。我们会在 24 小时内处理举报：删除违规内容，并封禁发布者的账号。多人举报的内容会被自动隐藏，等待审核。

4. 你的内容

你对自己发布的内容负责，并保留其所有权。你授权 Vegan+ 在 App 内展示这些内容。你可以随时删除自己的帖子和评论，或注销账号。

5. 菜谱与餐厅信息

菜谱和餐厅信息仅供参考。请根据自身过敏和健康情况判断；餐厅信息可能过时，请以商家实际情况为准。

6. 账号

请妥善保管你的密码。违反本协议的账号可能被暂停或删除。

7. 变更

我们可能更新本协议，重要变更会在 App 内告知。

🌱 Vegan+ Terms of Use

Effective October 24, 2026

1. Agreement

By creating an account or using the Vegan+ community you agree to these terms and the Privacy Policy.

2. Zero tolerance for objectionable content

Vegan+ has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. Do not post:

  • hate, discrimination, harassment, threats or bullying;
  • sexual, violent, gory or otherwise inappropriate content;
  • spam, ads, scams, or impersonation;
  • illegal content, or content that infringes others' copyright or privacy;
  • misinformation that could harm health.

3. Reporting and enforcement

You can report any post or comment and block any user. We act on reports within 24 hours by removing the content and suspending the account that posted it. Content reported by several people is hidden automatically pending review.

4. Your content

You're responsible for what you post and you keep ownership of it. You allow Vegan+ to display it in the app. You can delete your posts and comments, or your account, at any time.

5. Recipes and places

Recipes and place information are for general reference. Check ingredients against your own allergies and health needs; place details may be out of date.

6. Your account

Keep your password safe. Accounts that break these terms may be suspended or deleted.

7. Changes

We may update these terms and will tell you in the app about important changes.
