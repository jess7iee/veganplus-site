常见问题

怎么注册和登录？

打开「社区」，点左上角的头像图标，用邮箱和密码注册。菜谱和附近功能不需要登录。

忘记密码怎么办？

请用注册邮箱发邮件给我们，我们核实后会帮你重置密码。

怎么注销账号、删除我的数据？

在 App 里进入「社区 → 个人资料 → 注销账号」，输入密码确认。账号和你发布的所有帖子、照片、评论都会被永久删除。

怎么举报帖子或屏蔽用户？

点帖子或评论右上角的「…」，选择「举报」或「屏蔽用户」。我们会在 24 小时内处理举报；被屏蔽用户的内容不会再出现在你的社区里。

"附近"找不到餐厅？

请在「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 定位服务 → Vegan+」中允许定位。素食店数据来自 OpenStreetMap 和 Apple 地图，有些地区数据较少；可以切换分类或下拉刷新。

收藏和"不吃的食材"会同步吗？

目前只保存在这台设备上。

菜谱有错误或想推荐菜谱？

欢迎发邮件告诉我们，或者在社区的「菜谱灵感」话题里分享。

FAQ

How do I sign up or sign in?

Open Community and tap the profile icon at the top left, then sign up with your email and a password. Recipes and Nearby work without an account.

I forgot my password.

Email us from the address you signed up with and we'll reset it for you after verifying the account.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Community → your profile → Delete account and confirm with your password. Your account and everything you posted (posts, photos, comments) are permanently deleted.

How do I report a post or block someone?

Tap "…" on any post or comment and choose Report or Block user. We act on reports within 24 hours, and you won't see a blocked user's posts or comments again.

Nearby doesn't find any places.

Allow location access in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Vegan+. Places come from OpenStreetMap and Apple Maps, and some areas have fewer listings — try another category or pull to refresh.

Do favorites and avoided ingredients sync?

Not yet — they're saved on this device only.

Found a mistake in a recipe, or want to suggest one?

Email us, or share it in the community under Recipe ideas.
