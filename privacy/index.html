🌱 Vegan+ 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

中文English用户协议 / Terms支持 / Support

我们收集什么

  • 账号信息：注册时的邮箱、昵称和头像表情。密码只以加密哈希保存，我们看不到你的密码。
  • 你发布的内容：社区帖子、照片、评论、点赞，以及你提交的举报和屏蔽名单。
  • 位置：只在你打开"附近"时，把当前位置发送到我们的服务器来查找附近的素食店。位置不会和你的账号关联，我们不保存位置历史；服务器的运行日志（会在几天内自动删除）中可能短暂包含请求里的坐标。
  • 收藏、"不吃的食材"等偏好：只保存在你的手机上。

我们怎么用

只用来提供 Vegan+ 的功能：登录、显示社区内容、处理举报和维护社区安全。我们不投放广告、不做跨 App 追踪、不出售任何数据。

谁能看到

你的昵称、头像和发布的内容对所有 Vegan+ 用户公开。你的邮箱不会公开。

存储与服务商

数据存储在 Cloudflare（数据库、图片存储和服务器日志）。附近餐厅信息来自 OpenStreetMap 和 Apple 地图；查询时会把位置发送给这些服务。

删除你的数据

在 App 的「社区 → 个人资料 → 注销账号」中可以随时永久删除账号，以及你发布的所有帖子、照片和评论。

儿童

Vegan+ 不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

变更与联系

政策有重要变更时会在 App 内告知。如有问题，请发邮件到 zwonderz@gmail.com，或访问支持页面。

🌱 Vegan+ Privacy Policy

Effective October 24, 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your email, display name and emoji avatar. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash — we can't see them.
  • What you post: community posts, photos, comments and likes, plus reports and blocks you make.
  • Location: when you open Nearby, your current location is sent to our server to find vegan places around you. It isn't linked to your account and we keep no location history; the coordinates may briefly appear in server logs, which are deleted automatically within days.
  • Favorites and "ingredients I avoid": stay on your device.

How we use it

Only to run Vegan+: signing in, showing the community, handling reports and keeping the community safe. We don't show ads, don't track you across apps, and never sell data.

Who can see it

Your display name, avatar and posts are visible to everyone using Vegan+. Your email is never shown.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Cloudflare (database, photo storage and server logs). Nearby places come from OpenStreetMap and Apple Maps, which receive your location when you search.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Community → your profile → Delete account to permanently delete your account and everything you posted.

Children

Vegan+ is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We'll let you know in the app about important changes. Questions? Email zwonderz@gmail.com or visit our support page.
